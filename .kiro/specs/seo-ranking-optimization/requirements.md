# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This feature optimizes the on-page and technical SEO of the TheClientPilot (TCP) website (https://theclientpilot.store) to maximize its search visibility for high-intent local queries such as "best AI agency", "best agency", "best AI agency in Guwahati" and "best AI agency in Assam", with a focus on local dominance across Guwahati, Assam and Northeast India.

The site is a single-page Next.js 16 (App Router) application deployed on Vercel. It already ships rich metadata, a large keyword set, and four JSON-LD blocks (Organization, WebSite, ProfessionalService/LocalBusiness, FAQPage). This feature closes the remaining technical and local-SEO gaps: crawler control files (robots.txt, sitemap.xml), a raster social/OG image, completeness of the LocalBusiness schema (geo, telephone, opening hours, social profiles), verified structured-data validity, semantic markup and image-accessibility auditing, Core Web Vitals performance, and a repeatable SEO scoring/baseline mechanism.

All optimizations operate strictly "under the hood." They MUST NOT alter the rendered visual appearance of the website and MUST NOT change any visible written copy. Improvements are limited to metadata, structured data, crawler-control files, non-rendering semantic attributes, performance, image alt attributes, and additive non-visible content.

This document also captures off-page and local-pack enablers as explicit requirements. These are stated realistically: on-page and technical SEO alone cannot guarantee a #1 ranking, which additionally depends on a Google Business Profile, reviews, backlinks and competitive factors outside the codebase. The measurable targets in this document apply to on-page/technical SEO quality and to enabling (not guaranteeing) local-pack and organic ranking.

## Glossary

- **Site**: The TheClientPilot public website served at https://theclientpilot.store.
- **SEO_System**: The collection of build-time and runtime mechanisms in the Next.js application responsible for producing SEO artifacts (metadata, structured data, robots, sitemap, images).
- **Crawler**: An automated search-engine or social-media agent that requests Site resources (for example Googlebot, Bingbot, or a social card scraper).
- **Robots_File**: The `/robots.txt` resource generated by the application (Next.js `app/robots.ts`).
- **Sitemap_File**: The `/sitemap.xml` resource generated by the application (Next.js `app/sitemap.ts`).
- **OG_Image**: The raster (PNG or JPG) social-sharing image referenced by OpenGraph and Twitter Card metadata, sized 1200x630 pixels.
- **Structured_Data**: The JSON-LD blocks embedded in the document describing the Organization, WebSite, LocalBusiness/ProfessionalService and FAQPage entities.
- **LocalBusiness_Schema**: The JSON-LD block typed as `ProfessionalService` and `LocalBusiness` describing TCP as a local business.
- **Metadata**: The Next.js `Metadata` export in `layout.tsx` producing title, description, keywords, canonical, robots, OpenGraph and Twitter tags.
- **Semantic_Markup**: HTML landmark and heading structure (`main`, `header`, `nav`, `h1`–`h6`) and non-visible accessibility attributes that do not change rendered layout.
- **Core_Web_Vitals**: The Google-defined performance metrics Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) and Interaction to Next Paint (INP).
- **SEO_Score**: The Lighthouse SEO category score (0–100) measured for the Site.
- **SEO_Audit**: A repeatable, documented measurement run that records SEO_Score, Core_Web_Vitals and Structured_Data validity at a point in time.
- **Baseline_Audit**: The first SEO_Audit, recorded before optimization changes, used as the comparison reference.
- **Visible_Content**: Any text or graphic that is rendered and perceivable by a sighted user in the browser viewport.
- **GBP**: Google Business Profile, the external listing that powers local-pack results.
- **Search_Console**: Google Search Console, the external property used to verify site ownership and monitor search performance.
- **Target_Queries**: The prioritized search phrases "best AI agency", "best agency", "best AI agency in Guwahati", "best agency in Guwahati", "best AI agency in Assam" and "best agency in Assam".

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Preserve Visuals and Visible Content

**User Story:** As the site owner, I want every SEO change to leave the design and copy untouched, so that the brand experience is not degraded while search visibility improves.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an SEO optimization is applied to a Site page, THE SEO_System SHALL preserve the rendered visual appearance such that a pixel-by-pixel comparison of the pre-change and post-change rendered output produces zero differing pixels at each supported viewport width (320 pixels to 1920 pixels wide).
2. WHEN an SEO optimization is applied to a Site page, THE SEO_System SHALL preserve all Visible_Content such that no rendered text or graphic perceivable by a sighted user at any supported viewport width (320 pixels to 1920 pixels wide) is added, removed, or modified.
3. WHERE an optimization adds content for Crawler consumption, THE SEO_System SHALL render that content such that it occupies zero visible layout space and introduces zero differing pixels in the rendered output at each supported viewport width, using screen-reader-only or metadata mechanisms.
4. WHERE an optimization changes Semantic_Markup, THE SEO_System SHALL apply only attributes and element roles that introduce zero differing pixels in the rendered output at each supported viewport width.
5. IF a pre-implementation comparison determines that an optimization would produce one or more differing pixels in the rendered output or would add, remove, or modify Visible_Content at any supported viewport width, THEN THE SEO_System SHALL exclude that optimization from implementation and retain the pre-change rendering.
6. IF an applied optimization is detected to produce one or more differing pixels in the rendered output or a change to Visible_Content at any supported viewport width, THEN THE SEO_System SHALL revert that optimization, restore the pre-change rendered output, and record an indication that the optimization was rejected for causing a visual change.

### Requirement 2: Robots File for Crawler Control

**User Story:** As a search engine crawler, I want a robots directive, so that I can discover which resources to crawl and locate the sitemap.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Crawler requests `/robots.txt`, THE Robots_File SHALL return an HTTP 200 response with a `Content-Type` of `text/plain` within 1000 milliseconds.
2. THE Robots_File SHALL declare a directive applicable to all crawler user agents (wildcard `*`) that permits crawling of all Site resources except those explicitly disallowed under criterion 5.
3. THE Robots_File SHALL declare the Sitemap_File location as the absolute URL `https://theclientpilot.store/sitemap.xml`.
4. THE Robots_File SHALL declare the canonical host as the absolute URL `https://theclientpilot.store`.
5. THE Robots_File SHALL disallow crawling of non-public resource paths, including build artifacts and internal API routes.
6. IF the Robots_File content cannot be generated, THEN THE Robots_File SHALL return an HTTP 500 response with a `text/plain` body indicating the generation failure, without returning partial or malformed directives.

### Requirement 3: Sitemap File for Indexing

**User Story:** As a search engine crawler, I want an XML sitemap, so that I can index the Site's canonical URLs efficiently.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Crawler requests `/sitemap.xml`, THE Sitemap_File SHALL return an HTTP 200 response within 1000 milliseconds with a body that is well-formed XML conforming to the sitemaps.org protocol schema and declaring the UTF-8 character encoding.
2. WHEN a Crawler requests `/sitemap.xml`, THE Sitemap_File SHALL set the response content type to an XML media type (`application/xml`).
3. THE Sitemap_File SHALL list exactly one URL entry containing the canonical URL `https://theclientpilot.store` for the home page.
4. THE Sitemap_File SHALL include a last-modified date for each listed URL formatted as a W3C Datetime value conforming to ISO 8601 (for example, `2024-01-01` or `2024-01-01T00:00:00+00:00`).
5. WHERE a URL is included in the Sitemap_File, THE Sitemap_File SHALL express that URL in absolute canonical form using the `https` scheme and the host `theclientpilot.store` with no trailing slash beyond the domain root.
6. IF generation of the sitemap content fails, THEN THE Sitemap_File SHALL return an HTTP 5xx server error response and SHALL NOT return a partial or malformed XML body.

### Requirement 4: Raster Social Sharing Image

**User Story:** As a person sharing the Site on social media or seeing it in search previews, I want a correctly sized preview image, so that the link renders with a proper visual card.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE SEO_System SHALL provide an OG_Image encoded in either PNG or JPG format, with dimensions of exactly 1200 pixels wide by 630 pixels high and a file size not exceeding 5 megabytes.
2. THE Metadata SHALL reference the OG_Image as the OpenGraph image using a fully-qualified absolute URL that begins with the `https://` scheme and includes the complete host and path.
3. THE Metadata SHALL reference the OG_Image as the Twitter Card image using a fully-qualified absolute URL that begins with the `https://` scheme and includes the complete host and path.
4. THE Metadata SHALL declare the OG_Image width as 1200, height as 630, and a non-empty alt attribute of at most 420 characters describing TheClientPilot.
5. WHEN a Crawler requests the OG_Image URL, THE SEO_System SHALL return, within 2 seconds, an HTTP 200 response with a content type of `image/png` or `image/jpeg`.
6. IF a request for the OG_Image URL cannot be served successfully, THEN THE SEO_System SHALL return an HTTP error response with a status code of 400 or greater, without returning image content typed as `image/png` or `image/jpeg`.

### Requirement 5: Complete LocalBusiness Structured Data for Local Ranking

**User Story:** As a person searching for an AI agency in Guwahati or Assam, I want TCP's business details to be complete and machine-readable, so that TCP is eligible to appear in local search and map results.

**Assumption/Dependency:** The exact street address, telephone number, geo coordinates, opening hours, and social/business profile URLs are owner-provided values. Until the owner supplies a given value, that value is considered unavailable and is governed by criterion 8 (property omission), so the emitted schema always remains valid.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE LocalBusiness_Schema SHALL include a postal address containing a street or locality detail field, an `addressLocality` of Guwahati, an `addressRegion` of Assam, a postal code, and an `addressCountry` of IN.
2. THE LocalBusiness_Schema SHALL include a `geo` property with a numeric `latitude` between -90 and 90 and a numeric `longitude` between -180 and 180, expressed in decimal degrees, identifying the Guwahati business location.
3. THE LocalBusiness_Schema SHALL include a `telephone` property in international E.164 format, consisting of a leading `+`, a country code, and a total of no more than 15 digits.
4. THE LocalBusiness_Schema SHALL include an `openingHoursSpecification` property in which each entry declares one or more `dayOfWeek` values from Monday through Sunday and `opens` and `closes` times in 24-hour HH:MM notation.
5. THE LocalBusiness_Schema SHALL include a `sameAs` property whose value is a list of absolute URLs, each beginning with an https scheme and a host, referencing TCP's public social and business profiles.
6. THE LocalBusiness_Schema SHALL reference the OG_Image or another raster image via its `image` property using an absolute URL beginning with an https scheme and a host.
7. THE LocalBusiness_Schema SHALL declare `areaServed` covering Guwahati, Assam, Northeast India, and India.
8. IF an owner-provided value for the `address` street detail, `telephone`, `geo`, `openingHoursSpecification`, or `sameAs` property is unavailable, THEN THE LocalBusiness_Schema SHALL omit that property entirely rather than emit it with an empty, null, or placeholder value.
9. WHEN the LocalBusiness_Schema is generated, THE LocalBusiness_Schema SHALL contain only properties whose values conform to the format and range constraints stated in criteria 1 through 7, so that the emitted schema validates without format errors.

### Requirement 6: Structured Data Validity

**User Story:** As a search engine, I want all structured data to be valid and error-free, so that I can trust and use it for rich results and local ranking.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Structured_Data SHALL conform to the schema.org vocabulary for each declared entity type (Organization, WebSite, LocalBusiness/ProfessionalService, and FAQPage), including all properties that schema.org marks as required for that type.
2. WHEN Structured_Data is validated with a schema validation tool, THE Structured_Data SHALL report zero errors across all four entity-type blocks.
3. IF a Structured_Data block is missing a property that schema.org marks as required for its declared entity type, THEN THE Structured_Data validation SHALL fail with an error identifying the entity type and the missing property.
4. THE Structured_Data SHALL express every `url`, `logo`, `image`, and `sameAs` value as an absolute URL that begins with the `https://` scheme and includes a fully qualified domain.
5. THE Structured_Data SHALL declare identical business name, postal address (street, locality, region, postal code, country), and primary telephone values across the Organization and LocalBusiness/ProfessionalService blocks, with zero character-level differences after trimming leading and trailing whitespace.
6. IF a business name, address, or contact value differs between the Organization and LocalBusiness/ProfessionalService blocks, THEN THE Structured_Data validation SHALL fail with an error identifying the inconsistent property and both conflicting values.
7. WHEN a Structured_Data property references an image, THE Structured_Data SHALL reference a raster image resource (JPEG, PNG, or WebP) whose URL returns HTTP 200 within 5 seconds.
8. IF an image resource referenced by Structured_Data returns any status other than HTTP 200 or does not respond within 5 seconds, THEN THE Structured_Data validation SHALL fail with an error identifying the unreachable image URL.

### Requirement 7: Metadata Optimization Within Constraints

**User Story:** As a search engine, I want accurate and non-duplicated page metadata, so that I can index and rank the Site correctly for Target_Queries.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Metadata SHALL declare exactly one canonical URL equal to `https://theclientpilot.store` for the home page.
2. IF more than one canonical URL is declared for the home page, or a declared canonical URL differs from `https://theclientpilot.store`, THEN THE SEO_System SHALL flag the conflicting or duplicate canonical declaration as non-compliant.
3. THE Metadata SHALL declare a rendered title of between 1 and 60 characters inclusive and a rendered meta description of between 1 and 160 characters inclusive, measured as rendered character counts.
4. IF the source copy for the title or meta description exceeds its character limit, THEN THE SEO_System SHALL truncate the affected metadata value to its limit without altering any visible on-page content.
5. THE Metadata SHALL declare `robots` directives that permit both indexing and following for the home page.
6. THE Metadata SHALL declare a `lang` attribute of `en` on the root HTML element.
7. THE Metadata SHALL include exactly one Google site-verification token for Search_Console ownership verification.
8. IF more than one Google site-verification declaration is present, THEN THE SEO_System SHALL flag the duplicate site-verification declaration as non-compliant.

### Requirement 8: Semantic Markup and Heading Hierarchy Audit

**User Story:** As a search engine, I want a clear document structure with a single primary heading, so that I can understand the page's topic hierarchy.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Site SHALL expose exactly one `h1` element (element count equal to 1) in the rendered document.
2. THE Site SHALL expose exactly one `main` landmark element in the rendered document.
3. THE Site SHALL order heading levels so that, when descending, no heading is more than one level deeper than the nearest preceding heading.
4. THE Site SHALL preserve the rendered visual appearance during any Semantic_Markup correction, where rendered visual appearance is defined by the computed text content, on-screen position, rendered dimensions, typography, and color of each affected element.
5. WHERE the rendered document currently contains more than one `h1` element (for example the two visible `h1` elements in `Hero.tsx` and the screen-reader-only `h1` in `page.tsx`), THE SEO_System SHALL demote all but one `h1` to a heading level that preserves sequential heading order while retaining the rendered visual appearance and Visible_Content of each demoted element.
6. WHERE demoting an element to satisfy the single-`h1` requirement would otherwise change the rendered visual appearance, THE SEO_System SHALL preserve the element's original styling and visible text so that the computed text, position, dimensions, typography, and color remain identical.
7. WHERE a redundant screen-reader-only `h1` exists, THE SEO_System SHALL remove or demote that element with zero change to rendered visual appearance and zero change to Visible_Content.

### Requirement 9: Image Accessibility and Alt Attributes

**User Story:** As a search engine and an assistive-technology user, I want every meaningful image to have descriptive alternative text, so that image content is indexable and accessible.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Site SHALL provide a non-empty `alt` attribute, containing between 1 and 125 characters that describe the image's content or function, for every content image (an image that conveys information to the user).
2. WHERE an image is decorative (an image that conveys no information and exists only for visual styling), THE Site SHALL provide an `alt` attribute whose value is an empty string so that assistive technology skips it.
3. WHEN an `alt` attribute is added or modified, THE SEO_System SHALL leave the rendered visual appearance unchanged, keeping the image's rendered dimensions, position, and all other visible pixels identical to the state before the change.
4. IF a content image is missing an `alt` attribute or has an empty `alt` attribute, THEN THE SEO_System SHALL flag that image as non-compliant and report an indication identifying the affected image, without altering the image's rendered output.

### Requirement 10: Core Web Vitals Performance

**User Story:** As a site visitor and a search engine, I want the Site to load and respond quickly, so that ranking is not penalized and users are not frustrated.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Lighthouse mobile lab audit (simulated mobile device with default mobile network and CPU throttling) is run against any indexable page of the Site, THE Site SHALL achieve a Largest Contentful Paint of at most 2.5 seconds on that page.
2. WHEN a Lighthouse mobile lab audit is run against any indexable page of the Site, THE Site SHALL achieve a Cumulative Layout Shift of at most 0.1 on that page.
3. WHEN a Lighthouse lab audit is run against any indexable page of the Site, THE Site SHALL achieve an Interaction to Next Paint (or its lab proxy) of at most 200 milliseconds on that page.
4. THE Site SHALL serve every content image in a next-generation image format (WebP or AVIF).
5. IF a performance optimization would change rendered visual appearance or Visible_Content, THEN THE SEO_System SHALL exclude that optimization and retain the current rendered output unchanged.
6. THE Site SHALL render every content image with explicit width and height attributes whose values match the image's intrinsic aspect ratio, so that the image reserves layout space before it loads.
7. IF a content image cannot be served in a next-generation format, THEN THE Site SHALL serve the image in its original format without altering its rendered dimensions or visual appearance.

### Requirement 11: SEO Scoring and Baseline Mechanism

**User Story:** As the site owner, I want a repeatable way to measure SEO quality, so that I can establish a baseline and confirm improvements.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN an SEO_Audit is run, THE SEO_Audit SHALL measure the SEO_Score (0 to 100), the Core_Web_Vitals values, and the Structured_Data validity result for every indexable page of the Site.
2. WHEN an SEO_Audit completes, THE SEO_System SHALL record the measured SEO_Score, Core_Web_Vitals values, and Structured_Data validity result with a timestamp identifying the audit run.
3. THE SEO_System SHALL classify the Structured_Data validity result as valid only when zero structured-data errors are reported for the audited page.
4. WHEN the SEO_System runs the first SEO_Audit before any optimization changes are applied, THE SEO_System SHALL record that audit as the Baseline_Audit.
5. WHEN an SEO_Audit is run after optimization, THE SEO_System SHALL record the post-optimization SEO_Score and present it alongside the Baseline_Audit SEO_Score for comparison.
6. IF an SEO_Audit fails to complete or does not produce an SEO_Score, THEN THE SEO_System SHALL retain any previously recorded audit results unchanged and return an error indication describing the failure.
7. THE Site SHALL achieve a post-optimization SEO_Score of at least 95 out of 100 for every audited page.
8. IF a post-optimization SEO_Score is below 95 out of 100, THEN THE SEO_System SHALL record the result as a failed target and return an indication that the minimum SEO_Score was not met.
9. THE SEO_Audit SHALL be re-runnable using a documented command or procedure, and repeated runs against an unchanged Site SHALL produce SEO_Score values that differ by no more than 3 points.

### Requirement 12: Off-Page and Local-Pack Enablers

**User Story:** As the site owner, I want the technical prerequisites for local-pack and organic ranking in place, so that off-page factors such as GBP, reviews and backlinks can drive TCP toward the top for Target_Queries.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE LocalBusiness_Schema SHALL expose the business name, postal address, telephone contact fields, and a non-empty `sameAs` property required to associate the Site with a GBP listing.
2. IF the LocalBusiness_Schema is missing the business name, postal address, telephone, or a non-empty `sameAs` value needed for GBP association, THEN THE SEO_System SHALL return a per-field validation indication identifying each missing field.
3. THE SEO_System SHALL provide Search_Console ownership verification so that indexing status and Target_Queries performance can be monitored.
4. THE SEO_System SHALL document, as separate documented items, the off-page actions required to pursue local-pack ranking, comprising GBP creation, review acquisition, and local citation building.
5. THE SEO_System SHALL document that a #1 ranking for Target_Queries cannot be guaranteed by on-page changes alone, enumerating the off-page factors it depends on, comprising the Google Business Profile, customer reviews, backlinks, and competition.
6. WHEN the Site is submitted to Search_Console, THE SEO_System SHALL provide the Sitemap_File absolute URL `https://theclientpilot.store/sitemap.xml` for submission.
7. IF the Sitemap_File is unavailable at submission time, THEN THE SEO_System SHALL surface an indication of the unavailability rather than submitting a broken URL.
