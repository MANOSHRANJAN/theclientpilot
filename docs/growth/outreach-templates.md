# Outreach templates

One mention in a real publication is worth more than every directory listing
combined. Directories are links anyone can get; press is a link that says someone
vouched for you.

These are drafts, not scripts. Change the wording so it sounds like you — a mail
that reads like a template gets deleted like one.

**Rules for all of them:** short, no attachments, one clear ask, real subject line.
Send from `theclientpilot@outlook.com`. Follow up **once** after a week, then stop.

---

## 1. College / campus press

The easiest yes you will get. Campus publications and startup cells actively look
for student founders, and you are one.

**Send to:** your college's startup cell / E-cell, campus newsletter editor,
placement or alumni office.

**Subject:** Student running an AI agency — worth a campus feature?

```
Hi <name>,

I'm Manosh, a student here. Alongside classes I run TheClientPilot, an AI
agency that builds systems for clinics and local businesses — AI receptionists
that answer calls the clinic misses, and automated follow-up over WhatsApp.

I'm from Guwahati and started this because clinics back home were losing
patients purely because nobody could pick up the phone during a consultation.

If the newsletter ever covers student founders, I'd be happy to write something
up or answer questions. Site is theclientpilot.store if you want a look.

Thanks,
Manosh Ranjan
```

## 2. Northeast / Assam media

"Guwahati student builds AI agency" is a story these outlets publish regularly.
Lead with the local angle, not the technology.

**Send to:** Assam Tribune, Northeast Now, G Plus Guwahati, Time8, EastMojo.
Find the reporter who covers business or startups and mail them directly —
never `info@`.

**Subject:** Guwahati student building AI systems for local clinics

```
Hi <name>,

I read your piece on <specific article>. I thought this might fit what you cover.

I'm Manosh Ranjan, from Guwahati, currently studying in Sonipat. I run
TheClientPilot, building AI receptionists for clinics — software that answers
calls a clinic misses and books the appointment automatically.

The reason I started: clinics in Guwahati lose patients every day for a boring
reason. The phone rings while the doctor is with someone, nobody picks up, and
the patient books with whoever answered first. Most of them have no idea how
many they lose.

Happy to talk it through, or share what I'm seeing across the practices I work
with, if it's useful for a story.

theclientpilot.store

Thanks,
Manosh
```

## 3. Journalist request platforms

Reporters post questions daily and need sources fast. Answer well and you get
quoted with a link.

**Where:** Qwoted, Featured.com, SourceBottle. Free to sign up as an expert.

**What to watch for:** queries on AI adoption in small business, marketing
automation, healthcare marketing, Indian startups, student entrepreneurship.

**How to answer:**

- Reply within an hour. Speed beats polish — most queries close in a day.
- Give a specific, concrete answer they can quote directly. Not "AI is
  transformative" — say what you actually see clinics doing and what it costs them.
- Two or three sentences. They want a quote, not an essay.
- Sign as: `Manosh Ranjan, Founder, TheClientPilot (theclientpilot.store)`

## 4. Client case study

After a project, ask for two things at once. Both are worth more than a link.

**Subject:** Quick favour — and your results so far

```
Hi <name>,

Wanted to share where things stand: <specific result — calls answered,
appointments booked, response time>.

Two small asks if you're happy with how it's gone:

1. Would you leave a Google review? Takes a minute and it genuinely helps
   people find us. <link>

2. Could I write up what we built as a case study on my site? I'd send it to
   you before anything goes live.

And if you're open to it, a small "Website by TheClientPilot" line in your
footer — no pressure at all if you'd rather not.

Thanks,
Manosh
```

## Tracking

Keep a simple sheet: who, when contacted, replied yes/no, followed up, link live.
Outreach is a numbers game — ten sends, one or two links, and that is a normal,
healthy hit rate. Do not read a silence as a rejection of the business.
