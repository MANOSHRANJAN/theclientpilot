### Address

TheClientPilot is a **service area business** — it serves clients across a region
and has no premises the public can visit.

When a form demands a street address:

1. Look for a "I serve customers at their location" / "hide my address" option and
   use it. Google Business Profile, Justdial and most serious directories have one.
2. If there is no such option, **leave the listing rather than inventing an
   address**. A fabricated or residential address that contradicts your other
   listings is worse than no listing at all, and on Google Business Profile it is
   grounds for suspension.

Never publish the founder's family home address.

## Rules

- **Never buy links.** No "1000 backlinks" packages, no PBNs, no paid guest-post
  networks. A penalty on a domain this young is very hard to reverse, and these
  packages are the exact pattern Google's spam systems are built to catch.
- **Never submit the same listing twice.** Duplicates split your signal and some
  directories ban for it.
- **Quality beats volume.** Fifteen relevant links in six months is a healthy,
  natural profile for a new agency. A hundred in a week is a red flag.
- **Anchor text stays branded.** See `client-site-badge.md` for why this matters.
