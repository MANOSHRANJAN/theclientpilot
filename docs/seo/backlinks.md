# Backlink Acquisition Playbook — TheClientPilot

Companion to `off-page-enablers.md`, which covers Google Business Profile, reviews
and citations. This document covers **backlinks** specifically.

## What cannot be automated

No code in this repository can create backlinks, and that is deliberate. Links
have to be earned or manually placed on sites you do not control.

Avoid all of the following. Each is a link-spam violation under Google's spam
policies and risks a manual action on a domain that has not yet established
trust — the downside is far worse than the upside:

- Paid link packages, "1000 backlinks" gigs, guaranteed-DA offers
- Private blog networks (PBNs)
- Automated directory submission blasts
- Blog comment, forum and profile link spam
- Reciprocal link schemes ("link to us and we'll link to you" at scale)
- Article spinning across low-quality syndication sites

A young domain with fifteen genuinely relevant local links will outrank one with
two thousand purchased links, and it will not be at risk of being deindexed.

## Priority order for TheClientPilot

Ranked by realistic return for an agency based in Guwahati, targeting Assam
first and Delhi NCR second.

### 1. Client case studies with reciprocal mentions

The strongest asset available. Each client you deliver results for is a
potential link from a real business in a relevant industry.

- Write up the result, ask the client to feature it or link from their site.
- A dental clinic linking to "our marketing partner" is a topically relevant
  local link — exactly the kind that moves local rankings.
- This also feeds the case-study content that converts future prospects.

### 2. Local business ecosystem

- Guwahati and Assam chamber of commerce / trade body membership pages
- Local startup and entrepreneur community directories
- Coworking space member listings
- Event sponsor or speaker pages for local business events

These are low-volume, high-relevance, and hard for competitors to replicate.

### 3. Industry and niche directories

Only ones with genuine editorial standards and real traffic. Indian and global
agency directories such as Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush and Sortlist accept
verified agency listings. Register properly with real details rather than mass
submitting.

### 4. Digital PR grounded in your own data

You operate AI receptionists, which means you can measure things nobody else in
your market can. That is genuinely newsworthy material:

- Percentage of clinic calls that go unanswered during working hours
- After-hours enquiry volume for healthcare businesses
- Average time-to-first-response across local service businesses
- Booking rate difference between instant reply and one-hour reply

Anonymise and aggregate, publish as a small study, then pitch to regional
business press, Northeast India news outlets and Indian healthcare/marketing
publications. One picked-up study can produce more link value than months of
manual outreach.

### 5. Guest contributions

Write for Indian marketing, healthcare-business and small-business publications.
The bar: would you write this article if it carried no link? If not, it is not
good enough to place.

### 6. Educational and student ecosystem

Given the Sonipat / Delhi NCR study base, university entrepreneurship cells,
student startup showcases and campus incubator pages are legitimate, accessible
link sources with real editorial oversight.

## Anchor text

Keep it natural and varied. Repeating an exact-match commercial phrase such as
"best AI agency in Assam" across every inbound link is a recognisable manipulation
pattern.

Aim for a mix roughly along these lines:

- Brand: "TheClientPilot", "theclientpilot.store" — the majority
- Natural phrases: "their AI receptionist", "marketing partner", "read more"
- Partial match: "AI agency in Guwahati" — occasional
- Exact commercial match: rare

## Internal linking (fully in your control)

Already implemented in code: every landing page links to the others and to the
home page, and the footer links to all landing pages sitewide. When new pages are
added to `src/lib/landing-pages.ts` they inherit this automatically.

Internal links are the one part of link equity you control completely. Use them.

## Measurement

Track in Google Search Console (Links report) and optionally Ahrefs or Semrush:

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| Referring domains | Unique domains matter far more than total link count |
| Link relevance | Local + industry-relevant links outweigh high-DA irrelevant ones |
| Anchor distribution | Watch for over-optimisation on commercial phrases |
| Landing page spread | Links should reach the location pages, not only the home page |

## Realistic expectations

Backlinks are the slowest-compounding part of SEO. Expect three to six months
before link acquisition visibly affects ranking for competitive phrases, and
longer for Delhi NCR terms than for Assam ones. Local pack visibility in Guwahati
will respond faster to Google Business Profile and review activity than to links.

Sequence accordingly: GBP and reviews first, then Assam content and links, then
Delhi NCR.
